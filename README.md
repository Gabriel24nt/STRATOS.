## 🇬🇧 1. Project Overview

STRATOS is a strategic intelligence platform designed to support decision-makers through the analysis of geopolitical, economic, financial and country-risk indicators.

The platform aims to centralize relevant information, transform data into actionable insights and facilitate strategic decision-making.

Its ambition is to provide an analytical environment inspired by professional economic intelligence and financial research platforms.

## 🇫🇷 2. Présentation du projet

STRATOS est une plateforme d'intelligence stratégique conçue pour accompagner les décideurs dans l'analyse des environnements géopolitiques, économiques et financiers.

Elle vise à centraliser des informations stratégiques, analyser les risques pays et transformer les données en informations exploitables pour la prise de décision.

L'objectif est de développer un outil analytique combinant intelligence économique, analyse financière, géopolitique et aide à la décision.

## 🌍 3. Core Modules | Modules principaux

### 01 — Strategic Dashboard
Centralized overview of strategic indicators and intelligence information.

### 02 — Intelligence Feed
Collection and management of economic, financial and geopolitical intelligence.

### 03 — Country Risk Monitor
Analysis of country-level indicators, including:
- GDP Growth
- Inflation
- Unemployment
- External Debt

### 04 — Global Intelligence Map
Geographical visualization of countries and strategic information.

### 05 — Financial Intelligence
Financial and macroeconomic analysis to support investment decisions.

### 06 — Competitive Intelligence
Monitoring of companies, industries and competitive environments.

### 07 — Strategic Decision Lab
Analytical environment designed to support strategic assessment and scenario-based decision-making.

## 📊 5. Analytical Framework

STRATOS is structured around three analytical dimensions:

**Economic Intelligence**
- Macroeconomic monitoring
- Sector analysis
- Economic trends
- Competitive environment

**Geopolitical Intelligence**
- Political stability
- Geopolitical developments
- Country risk
- Strategic exposure

**Financial Intelligence**
- Financial indicators
- Investment environment
- Macroeconomic risk
- Financial decision support
